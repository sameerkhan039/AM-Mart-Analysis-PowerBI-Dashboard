# AM-Mart-Analysis-PowerBI-Dashboard
An interactive E-Commerce and Superstore retail analytics dashboard built in **Microsoft Power BI** for "AM Mart". The dashboard tracks comprehensive retail metrics including branch profitability/unit price, customer demographics, payment preferences, monthly sales distribution, and product line performance using a modern dark UI theme.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Advanced data transformation, relational data modeling, and custom dark UI dashboard design.
* **Interactive Filtering:** Dynamic branch-level slicers for localized branch insights.

---

## 🔍 Key Visuals & Analytical Breakdown

### 1. High-Level Business KPIs (Cards)
* **Sum of Rating:** Overall customer satisfaction and feedback score (34.79K).
* **Sum of Unit Price:** Aggregate transaction scale and revenue tracker (200.57K).

### 2. Branch & Regional Performance
* **Sum of Unit Price by Branch:** Donut chart breaking down performance across key store locations (*Brooklyn*, *Manhattan*, and *Queens*).

### 3. Customer Demographics & Segmentation
* **Sum of Unit Price by Gender:** Comparative bar chart highlighting purchasing trends between female and male shoppers.
* **Sum of Unit Price by Customer Type:** Donut chart segmenting transactions between *Normal* customers (56.35%) and registered *Members* (43.65%).

### 4. Payment Modes & Monthly Trends
* **Sum of Unit Price by Payment:** Analyzes payment method breakdown across *Ewallet*, *Cash*, and *Credit Card* with balanced distribution percentages (~31% to ~35%).
* **Sum of Unit Price by Month:** Time-series column chart showing monthly revenue fluctuations across the entire year (peaking strongly in April and May).

### 5. Product Line Performance
* **Sum of Rating by Product Line:** Bar chart tracking top-rated categories (*Food & Beverages* leading significantly).
* **Sum of Quantity by Product Line:** Volume-based ranking showing units sold per category (*Food & Beverages* and *Health & Beauty* leading the sales volume).

---

## 💡 Key Takeaway & Business Impact
This AM Mart dashboard delivers granular visibility into multi-branch retail operations. By tracking customer segmentation, preferred payment channels, and seasonal monthly trends, retail managers can fine-tune inventory distribution across branches like Brooklyn and Manhattan while tailoring marketing campaigns for loyal members versus normal walk-in customers.
